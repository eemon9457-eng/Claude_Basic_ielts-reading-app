## QUESTIONS 1-5
Type: Multiple Choice
Choose the correct letter A, B, C or D.

1. What was the main advantage of the printing press?
   A) It made books more beautiful
   B) It allowed mass production of texts
   C) It improved handwriting
   D) It reduced paper consumption

2. According to the passage, why were books expensive before the printing press?
   A) The materials were expensive
   B) They had to be copied by hand
   C) Printers charged high prices
   D) Paper was scarce

Type: True / False / Not Given

2. B
6. False
7. True
8. Not Given

## EXPLANATIONS
